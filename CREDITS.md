## Art and audio

All sprites and sounds are original, generated by `scripts/generate-assets.mjs`,
and released under CC0-1.0. Per-file entries live in
`public/assets/manifest.json`; `npm run assets` verifies coverage and size.

| Asset | What it is |
|---|---|
| `egara/sprites.png` | 5 frames at 16×16: fly, swatter, spray, spider, boss |
| `egara/*.wav` | synthesised sting, hit, boss, win, lose, and a loopable wing buzz |

## Fonts

System UI and monospace stack only; no web font is downloaded. Telugu text uses
the device's Telugu font (Noto Sans Telugu on Android and most Linux systems).
