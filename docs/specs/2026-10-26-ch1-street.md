## The pattern (reused for ch2–ch8)

Each level gets: (1) one signature sim mechanic, deterministic and blink-based
like hazards; (2) a research-grounded art pass (verified texture first,
license-marked filler second); (3) ambient life that never touches the sim;
(4) screenshot verification before ship. Sim additions must keep the
determinism suite green; budgets stay (game ≤500 KB gz, assets ≤15 MB).

## ch1 design

**Fantasy:** Gali runs petals across a living street at golden hour, the
morning after the locket night. Rom-com warmth before the fall.

**Mechanic — wind gusts:** deterministic blink wind zones push Gali (px/s
force, position-clamped like movement). One cross-breeze zone mid-street
teaches drift compensation; petals placed to reward riding gusts.

**Dressing (verified texture):** kirana + flower + tea-stall shopfronts with
striped awnings and real Telugu signs (పూలు · టీ · కిరాణా), yellow-black auto
drifter crossing periodically, wire tangles with crow silhouettes, marigold
swags between poles, glowing locket-window as the final landmark.
