## Premise (high)

A murdered young man is reborn as a housefly and, with the help of the woman he
loved, wages an escalating revenge campaign against a powerful industrialist —
a tiny underdog against a giant. Director S. S. Rajamouli framed it as
socio-fantasy on a David-vs-Goliath template, blending revenge, romance, comedy
and the occult. The fly has no voice and no superpowers — only fly abilities,
human cunning, and an ally who can build tiny things. [1][4][8]

## Characters (high)

- **The Fly (hero).** Persistent, mischievous, protective. No speech, no powers:
  expresses through movement. Game traits: acrobatic evasion, quick learning,
  tenacious harassment. Limitation is the point — fragile, needs the ally. [1][4]
- **The Maker (Bindu-inspired, original character).** Micro-artist turned
  ally-engineer: builds tiny weapons, sets traps, fights beside the hero.
  Game role: unlocks/crafting vendor, co-op NPC in climax. [1][6]
- **The Magnate (Sudeep-inspired, original character).** Entitled, status-driven,
  quick to rage; arc from confident predator to paranoid, ruined murderer.
  Game role: escalating antagonist; his mental state = difficulty/chaos dial
  (more traps, more guards, wilder attacks as "paranoia meter" rises). [1][4]
- **Supporting cast (original analogues):** the Exorcist (mid-boss), the Partner
  (betrayed ally — mansion infiltration motive), the Friend (tutorial voice),
  household guards/pests as enemy roster. [1]

## Adaptable set-pieces and craft (medium-high)

- **Scale contrast as core aesthetic:** macro-lens, probe-lens, 2000 fps
  slow-motion fly-eye views; the hero fly was designed with ~80% eye-face for
  expression. Game translation: giant-household levels, slow-mo dodge, big-eyed
  readable hero sprite. [1][7]
- **Needle duel:** sewing needle as spear; wound-and-retaliation rhythm maps to
  a stab/dodge boss mechanic. [1]
- **Final cannon run:** gunpowder coat → match ignition → cannon → steel ball →
  oxygen cylinder. A scripted three-input sacrifice sequence. [1]
- **Harassment loop:** crash, threat message, sleeplessness, financial ruin —
  a mission structure of escalating pranks with a visible paranoia meter. [1][5]
- **Sound motif:** the composer wove the real fly buzz into the score, pitched
  by scene emotion. Game translation: adaptive buzz layer tied to danger. [9]
- **Micro-art devices:** pencil-lead locket (tutorial goal), tiny armor/weapons
  (unlocks), wing amulet (NG+ charm). [1]

## Themes and reception (high, except box office)

- Themes: love beyond death, revenge, underdog vs power. Tone: funny,
  sentimental, action-packed, romantic, with occult shading. [1][8]
- Releases: Telugu + Tamil (Naan Ee) + Malayalam, ~1,100 screens, July 2012;
  Hindi (Makkhi) and Swahili (Inzi) versions followed. [1]
- Awards (verified): 2 National Film Awards (Best Telugu Film, Best Special
  Effects), 5 Filmfare South, 3 SIIMA, 8 Nandi, 9 Toronto After Dark wins. [10][11]
- Box office (~₹125-130 cr claimed): **unverified single-source** — do not cite
  on the hub. [1]
- Legacy: international cult acclaim; listed among decade-best foreign films by
  The Ringer (single-source, medium). [1]
